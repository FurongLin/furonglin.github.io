CURRENT RESEARCH

From a research question to an interactive prototype.

RESEARCH IN PROGRESS

Multimodal Communication and Trust in Human–Drone Collaboration

I am exploring how communication of intentions and uncertainty between a person and an intelligent drone may affect trust and collaborative task performance. The current prototype uses a simulated drone with an underslung grappling hook.

  • HRI
  • Multimodal interaction
  • Human–AI trust
Question
Can communication improve human trust and support better collaboration?
Current stage
Prototype exploration and study design; research questions and conditions are still evolving.
“

When a drone is uncertain, how should it ask a person for clarification?

A QUESTION UNDER EXPLORATION
Explore the communication modes+

Verbal interaction

Allowing a person to communicate task intentions to the drone verbally.

Non-verbal interaction

Exploring interaction when the drone does not communicate verbally.

Visual clarification

Using a simulated visual display to ask clarification questions when the drone is uncertain.

These are candidate study directions, rather than a final experimental design or established findings.

Related prototyping: a virtual-assistant prototype built with Unity and Meta Quest, involving NavMesh navigation, humanoid animation, Animation Rigging / inverse kinematics and Quest 3 passthrough.
